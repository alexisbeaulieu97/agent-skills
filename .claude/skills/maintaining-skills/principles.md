# maintaining-skills: principles

The outcomes a skill written or changed with maintaining-skills should show.
They state the owner's intent; the skill's wording may change in any way
that reaches them, and a change to the wording is judged against them.

- It improves an agent's judgment without replacing it: it helps in cases it
  does not name, and leaves alone decisions the agent makes well.
- It works without depending on any other skill.
- It states only what it is the source of truth for.
- It keeps its owner's conventions and preferences at the strength the owner
  gave them.
- A change to it touches one idea, so its effect shows in the next uses.
